How each number is worked out, and what not to do with it

Spread. Nvidia's closing percentage move minus the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index's. It is a one-day, one-benchmark comparison with no beta adjustment. The stock had also been falling for weeks before the news, so not all of the day's move is the scandal.

Market value. Shares outstanding from the latest quarterly filing (counted in August) times the closing prices. The share count will have moved a little since.

Ratio. The day's drop in market value divided by the memes in one published roundup, and by the likes that Know Your Meme reports for four posts. Likes were reported as "over" each figure within a day of posting, so they are floors and already out of date.

Index. Reported seconds of AI-generated video times a common list price per second, converted to GPU-hours at the H100 rental index price. Other AI clips are circulating without a reported length, and nobody knows how many attempts each took, so the true figure is higher by an unknown amount. Video list prices range from $0.07 to $0.40 a second and were collected in May 2026.

This is a joke page, not investment research. None of it is a view on the stock.
